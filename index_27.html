The Matching System

We Match Three Things First

Before personality, before photos, before anything else — we check that both careers can actually coexist after marriage. Nobody should put their career on hold for love.

🗺️

1. Transfer Compatibility

RBI transfers every 5 years at regional offices, 10 years in Mumbai. SBI transfers every 2–3 years anywhere in India. AAI posts at specific airports. We show transfer windows for every organisation so you know upfront if your careers can stay in the same city.

REAL EXAMPLE

RBI employee in Mumbai + SBI employee in Mumbai = high transfer compatibility. RBI employee in Chennai + AAI controller in Delhi = low compatibility without relocation discussion.

Personality Matching

Because Who You Are Matters More Than What You Earn

🧘

Introvert vs Extrovert

Do you recharge alone or with people? A homebody married to a social butterfly can work — but only if both know it upfront and respect it.

🤝

Agreeable vs Assertive

Do you naturally compromise or do you hold your ground? Neither is wrong — but two very assertive people need to know they are choosing that dynamic.

📋

Organised vs Spontaneous

One person plans holidays six months ahead. The other books a train the night before. This causes real friction. We surface it early.

🌱

Curious vs Practical

One always wants to try new things. The other trusts what already works. Complementary — but needs to be understood before marriage, not after.

😌

Calm vs Emotional

How you handle stress, arguments, and bad days matters enormously in marriage. We measure emotional stability honestly and respectfully.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

Family Closeness

Do you want to live near parents? Visit every weekend? Or have an independent household? This is one of the biggest sources of conflict in marriages. We ask it directly.

Career Compatibility

No Career Should Be Put On Hold For Love

Public sector jobs come with transfer obligations. Both partners need to be honest about this before committing. SWAYMVAR makes this conversation easy — every profile shows organisation, current posting city, and how open they are to relocation.

🗺️

Periodic Transfers Are Real

Most public sector organisations transfer employees every 3–5 years depending on the organisation and its internal policy. This is a reality of government and PSU jobs — and it needs to be part of any marriage conversation.

📍

Current City Is Shown

Every SWAYMVAR profile shows the member's current posting city. This is the starting point. Whether both can stay in the same city long term is a conversation every couple must have — we just make sure it happens early.

💬

Relocation Openness Declared

Members indicate on their profile how open they are to relocation — fully flexible, flexible within a zone, or needs to stay in current city. This surfaces a critical question before any emotional investment is made.

The SWAYMVAR Approach

We do not pretend to know every organisation's exact transfer rules. We ask members to be honest about their situation and what they are willing to adjust. Honesty before commitment saves heartbreak after.
