Career & Compatibility

Transfer Fit Guide

In public sector careers, where you are posted matters as much as who you are. SWAYMVAR helps you understand transfer patterns across organisations so you can find someone whose career is truly compatible with yours.

Why Transfer Compatibility Matters

🏛️

Different Transfer Cycles

RBI transfers every 3 years. IAS can be transferred anytime. Banks transfer every 2-3 years. If cycles don't align you could spend years in different cities.

🗺️

Posting Location Overlap

Some organisations post only to metro cities. Others post to remote districts. Knowing this upfront prevents major life conflicts after marriage.

📋

Spouse Posting Policy

Many PSUs and government organisations have spouse posting policies. Knowing these can help couples plan their postings together.

Organisation-wise Transfer Pattern

Based on typical service conditions. Always verify with your HR for current policy.

🏦

Reserve Bank of India (RBI)

Financial Regulator

High Compatibility

Transfer Frequency

Every 3 years

Posting Cities

Metro cities only

Spouse Policy

Considered on request

Best Match Orgs

SEBI, NABARD, Banks

RBI postings are limited to major cities (Mumbai, Delhi, Chennai, Kolkata, Bengaluru etc). Predictable transfer cycle makes it easier to plan. Strong spouse accommodation policy exists informally.

📊

SEBI

Securities Regulator

High Compatibility

Transfer Frequency

Every 2-3 years

Posting Cities

Mumbai, Delhi, regional

Spouse Policy

Limited offices

Best Match Orgs

RBI, NABARD, IRDAI

Limited office locations means transfers are between a small set of cities. Easier to co-locate with someone from another financial regulator. Mumbai-heavy posting makes it good match with banks and LIC.

🏛️

PSU Banks (SBI, PNB, BoB etc)

Public Sector Banking

Medium Compatibility

Transfer Frequency

Every 2-3 years

Posting Cities

Pan-India incl. rural

Spouse Policy

Varies by bank/grade

Best Match Orgs

LIC, other banks

⚠️ Bank transfers can be to any location including rural areas. Higher grades (Scale III+) get more control over postings. Best matches are with LIC officers or other bank officers who understand this lifestyle.

🛡️

LIC & Public Insurance

Life & General Insurance

Medium Compatibility

Transfer Frequency

Every 3-5 years

Posting Cities

Divisional offices

Spouse Policy

Considered formally

Best Match Orgs

Banks, PSU companies

LIC has a relatively stable transfer pattern with longer tenures. Divisional city postings cover major towns. Spouse transfer requests are taken more seriously than many PSUs.

⚡

Oil & Energy PSUs (IOCL, ONGC, NTPC etc)

Energy Sector

Needs Discussion

Transfer Frequency

Every 3-5 years

Posting Cities

Refineries incl. remote

Spouse Policy

Varies by location

Best Match Orgs

Other oil PSUs, NTPC

⚠️ Refinery and plant postings can be in remote locations (Mathura, Barauni, Numaligarh). This needs open discussion before marriage. Best match is with someone from a similar organisation who understands this lifestyle.

✈️

AAI & Air Traffic Control

Aviation Sector

Medium Compatibility

Transfer Frequency

Every 3-5 years

Posting Cities

Airports across India

Spouse Policy

Considered on request

Best Match Orgs

Other AAI, Air India

Airports exist in Tier 1, 2 and 3 cities. ATC postings can be in smaller airports. Best match is with another AAI officer who understands shift work and airport lifestyle.
