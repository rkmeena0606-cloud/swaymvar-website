1. Enrollment Process — How It Works

The SWAYMVAR enrollment follows a strict two-step process. No exceptions.

Step 1 — Waitlist Application (Invite Request)

  1. You submit your details on the waitlist form at swaymvar.in
  2. Our Concierge team reviews your application personally within 48 hours
  3. If approved you receive a unique invitation code at your official email address
  4. This code is personal, non-transferable, and valid for 7 days only

Step 2 — Profile Creation

  1. Only members with a valid approved invitation code may create a profile
  2. You must enter your unique code to access the profile creation page
  3. Profile creation without an approved invitation code is not permitted
  4. Attempting to bypass this process will result in permanent disqualification

Important: Submitting a waitlist application does not guarantee approval or membership. SWAYMVAR reserves the right to approve or decline any application without obligation to provide reasons.

SWAYMVAR operates on a peer-verification model. By applying, you expressly consent to the following:

2.1 Official Email Verification

  • You must provide your official organisational email address (e.g. name@rbi.org.in, name@sbi.co.in)
  • This email must be currently active and accessible by you
  • A verification link will be sent to this email — you must click it to confirm ownership
  • Personal Gmail, Yahoo, or Hotmail addresses are not accepted as primary verification

2.2 Peer Verification Consent

  • You consent to SWAYMVAR contacting a trusted colleague from your organisation to confirm your professional standing
  • The peer verifier will only be asked: "Does this person work at your organisation in the stated role?"
  • Your peer verifier's identity is never disclosed to you
  • Your peer verifier is bound by the same confidentiality as SWAYMVAR
  • No personal, financial, or sensitive information is shared with the peer verifier

2.3 Employee ID / Official Credentials

  • You may be asked to submit your employee ID, official appointment letter, or service record as supplementary verification
  • These documents are used solely for identity confirmation
  • Documents are encrypted, stored securely, and never shared with any other member or third party
  • Documents are deleted from our systems after verification is complete

By applying you confirm: You voluntarily consent to peer verification of your professional identity. You understand this is necessary to maintain the trust and safety of all members.
