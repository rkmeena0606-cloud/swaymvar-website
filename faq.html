Account & Privacy

How is my official email kept private? expand_more
We utilize a proprietary "Blind Relay" system for all official communications. Your professional email address is never visible to other members. Correspondence is routed through our secure servers, ensuring your identity remains protected until you explicitly choose to share personal contact details.
Can I hide my profile from specific organizations? expand_more
Yes. Under 'Privacy & Security' in your settings, you can define 'Visibility Filters'. You may block your profile from being seen by employees of specific companies or government departments to maintain workplace discretion.

Verification

What does a Peer Verification check involve? expand_more
Peer Verification is a gold-standard trust mechanism. It involves an anonymous cross-reference of your professional standing with at least three other verified members within your industry. This process confirms your professional identity and grade without revealing specific salary or role data.
How long does the verification process take? expand_more
Verification typically takes 48 to 72 business hours. Our dedicated concierge team manually reviews documentation to ensure the highest standards of integrity within the Circle.

Logistics & Fit

How do I manage my transfer compatibility settings? expand_more
Navigate to 'Transfer Fit Settings' in your dashboard. Here, you can specify your openness to relocation based on career opportunities, state preferences, or government cadre requirements. This helps our algorithm match you with professionals whose career trajectories align with yours.
