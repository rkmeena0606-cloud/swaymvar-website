SWAYMVAR Trust System

How Verification Works

Every SWAYMVAR member goes through a simple, dignified verification process before their profile goes live. No repeated steps. No extra forms.

The Complete Process

Three Simple Steps. Done Once.

1

Step 1 — Apply on Waitlist

You Submit Your Details

Fill the simple invite form on swaymvar.in with your name, official email, organisation, designation, and posting city. Takes 2 minutes.

What we check at this step:

✓ Official email domain (e.g. @rbi.org.in, @sbi.co.in)
✓ Organisation is in our active list
✓ LinkedIn profile matches stated designation

2

Step 2 — Concierge Reviews

A Human Reviews Your Application

Our Concierge team personally reviews every application within 48 hours. A trusted contact from your organisation confirms your employment. This is the peer verification — done once, during invite approval.

Peer confirmation means:

A trusted colleague from your organisation confirms: "Yes, this person works here in the stated role." Their identity is never disclosed. The entire check is confidential.

✓ If approved — you receive a unique invitation code at your official email

3

Step 3 — Create Your Profile

Enter Your Code and Complete Profile

Use your invitation code to unlock the profile creation form. Fill in your personality traits, life preferences, and posting details. Submit — and your profile is reviewed by Concierge and activated within 24 hours.

No re-verification needed. Your identity was confirmed in Step 2.
